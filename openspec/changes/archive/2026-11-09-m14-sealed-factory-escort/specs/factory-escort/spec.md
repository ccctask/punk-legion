## Purpose
封闭工厂供已完成私人基地东扩的玩家独行或与好友护送检修车。服务端按个人参与和终局结果结算每日物资与首胜扩地。

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Escort the inspection trolley alone or with a friend
The service SHALL offer a permanent non-occupiable neighboring factory to commanders who personally opened their eastern land. One player or two eligible friends SHALL be able to escort the same authoritative trolley, maneuver their own machines and see why the trolley is moving or stopped.

#### Scenario: Clear an obstruction and reach the factory
- **WHEN** an escort leaves the trolley or enemy interceptors block it
- **THEN** it stops, reports the reason and resumes after a friendly escort returns and the obstruction clears; a lone participant can complete the route.

#### Scenario: Personal participation and recovery
- **WHEN** one member leaves before the finish while the other reaches the gate, or the room reconnects during escort
- **THEN** the route and seats remain consistent, only qualifying finishers receive credit, each member receives their own result once, and machines are released.

#### Scenario: Repeat or replay delivery
- **WHEN** a player finishes repeatedly on the same opening UTC day or a receipt is redelivered
- **THEN** the activity credits at most one structure within inventory limits, independently of all Rustrail and pump progression.
